# Cooking

- **C1** A **recipe** has a name (trimmed, 1 to 80 characters, unique **ignoring case**: `Soup` and ` soup ` are the same name; a recipe may be renamed to another capitalisation of its own name) and a list of ingredient lines. The same rule applies when writing and editing. — `Recipe`, `RecipeNameTaken`, `EmptyRecipeName`, `RecipeNameTooLong`, `RecipeRepository::named`
- **C2** A recipe makes either **a meal** or **a prep**. A prep makes exactly one **output ingredient** (seitan from gluten, water and spices) and has a **yield**, the amount it adds to the stock of that ingredient each time it is made, above zero. Switching a recipe from one kind to the other is an ordinary edit. — `Recipe::meal`, `Recipe::prep`, `ZeroYield`
- **C3** A recipe needs **at least one line**, and each ingredient appears **once**. A line's amount is either **some** (to taste, no amount) or above zero; zero is refused. Lines keep the order they were written in. — `RecipeLine`, `Need`, `RecipeNeedsIngredient`, `IngredientListedTwice`, `ZeroQuantity`
- **C4** A recipe is **makeable** when every line is covered by what is in stock now. A line is covered by its ingredient or, failing that, by one of its **variants** (P16), the line's own ingredient first and then the main ingredient and the other variants by name. An ingredient covers the amount when its stock reaches it; any stock above zero is enough for a "some" line, for an ingredient tracked by hand (P7) and for a variant counted in another unit than the line. The lines are taken one after the other from the same stock, so two lines that use the same ingredient both count. A recipe that is not makeable can still be cooked, because the stock is an estimate. — `Planner`, `Plan`
- **C4b** A line that nothing in stock covers can still be reached **through preps**: when a prep (C2) makes the line's ingredient or one of its variants and that prep can be made, the recipe is **ready after a prep**: no line is missing, and the recipe lists the preps to make first (`preps`), deepest first (feed the starter, knead the dough, then bake the bread). A prep is followed through every level and made as many times as the amount needs; its ingredients come out of the same stock as the rest of the recipe, so butter used by the dough is no longer there for the filling. A prep never counts on itself, directly or through other preps. A line that neither the stock nor a prep can cover is **missing**. — `Planner`
- **C5** Editing a recipe compares its lines **per ingredient**: a line that stays keeps its row and takes the new amount and position, a new ingredient adds a line, a dropped one removes it. It is never cleared and re-added, because the database runs inserts before deletes and would trip over the one line per ingredient and recipe. A broken rule changes nothing. — `Recipe::rewrite`
- **C6** Deleting a recipe deletes its lines only: the ingredients and the meals already logged stay. — `RemoveRecipe`
- **C7** An ingredient used by a recipe line or made by a prep **cannot be deleted** (422, the message lists the recipe names). Once those recipes are deleted or rewritten without it, it can. Meal lines do not hold an ingredient back (C11). — `IngredientInUse`, `RecipeRepository::using`, P11
- **C8** A **meal** has a day, a type (breakfast, lunch, dinner or snack), an optional title (trimmed, 1 to 80 characters, blank means none), at least one **eater** (the same person counts once) and lines. It needs a title or at least one line, so a meal can be just "Takeaway pizza". It records who logged it and when. The history shows the title, or the line names when there is none. — `Meal`, `MealNeedsEater`, `EmptyMeal`, `MealTitleTooLong`
- **C9** The day is sent by the client: the device's local date, today by default (the sheet offers Today, Yesterday or any date), so the server has no time zone. The sheet presets the type from the hour, 05:00 to 10:59 breakfast, 11:00 to 14:59 lunch, 15:00 to 17:59 snack, dinner otherwise; this is a screen default, not a rule, and can be changed. All members are eaters by default and at least one stays on. — `mealType.js`, `MealSheet`
- **C10** **Cooking a recipe and logging a free meal are the same action**: `LogMeal`. A recipe only prefills the sheet (title = recipe name, every automatic line with an amount at the recipe amount) and **a meal carries no link to its recipe**. Lines that are tracked by hand, and lines without an amount, start at 0 and are marked "not deducted"; lines at 0 are not sent. A line covered by a variant (C4) is prefilled with the variant instead, at the recipe amount when they share the unit and at 0 ("not deducted") otherwise, so the person says how much of the bag they used. Each line shows a preview such as `6 → 3` (a shortfall is shown in a warning colour) and can be stepped, removed, or added with "+ ingredient". — `LogMeal`, `MealSheet`, `lines.js`
- **C11** Logging a meal **consumes every amount it is sent**, above zero, whatever the "tracked by hand" flag of the ingredient, so a spoonful of oil typed in is deducted. The stock **clamps at 0** (P5) and the meal is **never refused** for a shortfall. A meal line keeps a **snapshot** of the ingredient's name and unit, and its ingredient link is set to null when the ingredient is deleted, so the history stays readable. One line per ingredient. — `Meal::log`, `MealLine`, `Portion`
- **C12** **Deleting a meal** can put the ingredients back (`giveBack`, off unless asked: the API's `giveBack=1`; the confirm dialog's "Put ingredients back" switch is on by default). It restocks exactly what each line says it used, even when the stock had been clamped at the time, which is the truth the person gave, and skips the lines whose ingredient no longer exists. Without it the stock is left alone. Putting back past 999 units is refused. — `RemoveMeal`, `Meal::giveBack`
- **C13** **Making a prep** (`MakePrep`) consumes the amounts used, as C11 (the sheet prefills the recipe lines, "+ ingredient" adds more), and adds the amount **made**, above zero and the recipe's yield by default, to the output ingredient. It writes **no meal** and has no undo. Only a prep can be made; a meal recipe is refused. — `Recipe::make`, `PrepSheet`, `NotAPrep`
- **C14** The **history** lists the meals of the latest 30 distinct days that have meals, newest day first and, within a day, the latest logged first, as `{meals, before}`. `before` is the oldest day returned when older meals exist, `null` otherwise, and `GET /api/meals?before=<day>` returns the 30 days with meals strictly before that day ("Load older"). A day with no meal never counts. Meals are grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, then the date) with a type badge, the eaters (ordered by name), and their ingredients on demand. — `MealHistory`, `HistoryPage`
- **C15** Cooking and making send **relative** writes: what was used is subtracted from the stock as it is now, never a stale absolute value (see P8). The Cook page lists the makeable meal recipes, then the makeable preps, then the recipes **ready after a prep** (C4b), each with a "Make <prep>" button that opens the first prep to make; the recipe page names the preps to make first. It also shows a chip with the number of ingredients suggested for the groceries (running low and not homemade, see [groceries.md](groceries.md) G4) that leads to them. After a successful cook the sheet plays the one bold motion of the app (the pot bounces, the used stocks tick down, a steam puff rises); `prefers-reduced-motion` turns it off and shows the new stocks at once. — `CookPage`, `CookSuccess`
- **C16** The kitchen is shared (see [accounts.md](accounts.md) A3): everyone edits every recipe and meal. An unknown recipe, meal, ingredient or eater answers 404, a broken rule 422. Known limit: there are no row locks, so two phones cooking with the same ingredient within milliseconds can still lose one deduction (see P14).
