# Groceries

- **G1** The grocery cycle has three steps on two screens: **prepare** (pick what to buy), **shop** (tick what goes in the basket) and **put away** (say what was bought, and the stock goes up). `/groceries` shows the prepare step while there is no open list and the shop step while there is one; `/groceries/put-away` is the last step. — `GroceriesPage`, `PutAwayPage`
- **G2** There is **at most one open list** for the whole kitchen. A list exists only while it is open: it is created when shopping starts and **deleted** when the groceries are put away, so there is no history of lists. Starting while a list is open is refused (422, `GroceryListAlreadyOpen`), and the database backs this up with a unique column that is always true. — `GroceryList`, `GroceryListRepository::isOpen`, `StartGroceryListHandler`
- **G3** A list starts with **at least one item** (`EmptyGroceryList`, 422). An item is an ingredient plus a ticked flag, unticked at first, and an ingredient is listed **once**: starting with the same ingredient twice, or adding one that is already listed, does nothing. — `GroceryList`, `GroceryItem`
- **G4** An ingredient is **suggested** when it is running low (P6), is bought in the current month (P15) and is **not homemade**. Homemade means it is the output of a prep recipe (C2): seitan, made with the Make it button, is never suggested even when it is out, but it can still be added to the list by hand. The rule runs on the server and the ingredient list carries it as `suggested` (and `homemade`); the screen never recomputes it. — `Suggestion`, `RecipeRepository::outputIds`, `IngredientViews`
- **G5** The prepare step lists the suggested ingredients, **pre-ticked**, out of stock first and then by name, each with what is left (or an "Out" badge). Unticking one leaves it off the list. Any saved ingredient can be searched and added, or created on the spot with a stock of zero, and it comes in ticked. **Start shopping** is disabled while nothing is ticked. The Cook page's "N running low" chip counts the suggested ingredients and leads here (C15). — `GroceryPrepare`, `prepareRows`, `CookPage`
- **G6** Ticking sends the **value**, `ticked: true` or `ticked: false`, and never a toggle, so a double tap or a retried request cannot flip an item back. On the shop screen the tick is applied on screen at once and the row sinks to the bottom of its place, struck through; if the request fails the row goes back and the error is shown. An unknown item answers 404. — `GroceryList::tick`, `TickGroceryItem`, `useGroceryTicks`
- **G7** The shop screen groups the items by place (fridge, freezer, pantry), unticked first, then by name. A "+ Add" picker at the top adds any ingredient to the open list, and **Back home** leads to the put-away step. A tick made on one phone shows on the other after its next refresh (when the app regains focus, comes back online, or every minute while visible). — `GroceryShop`, `useSync`
- **G8** The put-away step lists the **ticked** items by place, each with a "bought" stepper that starts at **one unit** (12 twelfths), moves by a unit for pieces and by a quarter for the other units, and goes down to zero. A preview shows the stock before and after (`¼ → 1¼`). An amount of zero adds nothing. — `PutAwayPage`, `BoughtRow`, `boughtLine`
- **G9** Unticked items are **not bought**: they sit in a collapsed "Not bought" section, and ticking one there includes it with the same stepper. The "Add anything" picker adds an extra ingredient that was not on the list, or creates a new one with a stock of zero; extras get the same stepper. — `PutAwayPage`
- **G10** **Done** sends the bought amounts, above zero and at most once per ingredient, and one request does everything: **every bought amount is added to the stock** (ticked items and extras alike, as relative additions, P8) and **the list is deleted in the same flush**. Unticked items are dropped; they come back as suggestions if they are still low. An empty `bought` just discards the list. A broken rule (an amount of zero, an ingredient twice, a stock past 999 units) changes nothing and keeps the list. — `PutAwayGroceries`, `GroceryList::putAway`
- **G11** After a put-away the screen shows a toast and goes back to Groceries, which is the prepare step again. A put-away page opened when no list is open, or whose list another phone has just put away, goes back to Groceries too. — `PutAwayPage`
- **G12** Deleting an ingredient removes it from the open list (the database cascades the item); a list left without items stays open and can be filled or put away. Unknown ingredients answer 404 and a missing open list answers 404 on every write (`GET /api/grocery-list` answers 200 with `null`). — `GroceryItem`, `NotFound`
- **G13** The kitchen is shared (see [accounts.md](accounts.md) A3): anyone can start, tick, add to and put away the open list, and the list records who started it (`startedBy`). Known limit: two phones putting away at the same moment can lose one of the two (P14); the second one gets a 404 for the list that is already gone. — `GroceryListView`
