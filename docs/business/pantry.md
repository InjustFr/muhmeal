# Pantry

- **P1** An ingredient is something the kitchen keeps in stock: a name, the **place** it lives in (fridge, freezer or pantry), the **unit** it is counted in, a stock and a "running low" threshold. There is one place per ingredient and one stock per ingredient. — `Ingredient`, `Location`
- **P2** Quantities are counted in whole **units** and **fractions of a unit**. The unit is only a label: piece, bottle, jar, pack, bag, box, carton, can or tub. There are no grams or millilitres, and pieces take fractions too (½ lemon). — `Unit`
- **P3** A quantity is stored as an integer count of **twelfths** of a unit, so that ¼ ⅓ ½ ⅔ ¾ (3, 4, 6, 8 and 9 twelfths) add up and compare exactly: three thirds are exactly one unit, never 0.999. The API carries that integer (`33` is 2¾). A quantity goes from 0 to 999 units (0 to 11988 twelfths) and anything outside is refused; ⅛ cannot be represented. — `Quantity`, `QuantityOutOfRange`
- **P4** The screen shows a quantity with a fraction glyph where one exists (¼ ⅓ ½ ⅔ ¾ ⅙ ⅚, after the whole number: 2⅓) and `≈0.4` otherwise, with the decimal separator of the language. A piece is shown without its unit; every other unit follows the quantity, in the singular up to one in English and below two in French, in the plural otherwise (zero is plural in English, singular in French). — `quantity.js`
- **P5** The stock never goes below zero. Taking more than what is there (a "one less" tap, a change by a negative amount) empties it and is never refused, because the stock is an estimate. Going above 999 units is refused. — `Ingredient::adjust`, `Ingredient::consume`
- **P6** An ingredient is **running low** when its stock is at or below its threshold, and **out** when the stock is zero (an ingredient that is out is also running low). The threshold defaults to one piece for pieces and a quarter of a unit for every container, also when the unit changes and no threshold is given; it can be set from zero to 999 units. — `Ingredient::isRunningLow`, `Ingredient::isOut`, `Unit::defaultLowAt`
- **P7** An ingredient can be **tracked by hand** (olive oil, spices): cooking never deducts it automatically. Such an ingredient, like a recipe line that only says "some", is covered by any stock above zero; every other line is covered when the stock reaches the amount. — `Ingredient::covers`
- **P8** Writes that two phones may send at once are **relative**: the "one more" and "one less" buttons send `adjust by ±12` for the whole units they moved. Taps on the same ingredient are applied on screen at once and merged into a single request once the person has paused for about 400 ms; if the request fails the screen goes back to the stored stock and says so. Only an explicit "set the exact amount" (`SetStock`) and an edit of the ingredient send an absolute value, because someone who has just looked at the bottle is right. — `AdjustStock`, `SetStock`, `useStockAdjustments`
- **P9** An ingredient name is trimmed, has 1 to 60 characters and is unique **ignoring case** (`Milk` and ` milk ` are the same name; `Œufs` and `œufs` too). An ingredient may be renamed to another capitalisation of its own name. The same rule applies when adding and editing. — `IngredientNameTaken`, `EmptyIngredientName`, `IngredientNameTooLong`, `IngredientRepository::named`
- **P10** A new ingredient starts with the stock given when it is added (`stock`, 0 to 11988 twelfths, none given means 0); the add sheet proposes one unit and lets the person pick the whole number and a fraction, so "what I have and how much" is one step. Editing an ingredient (name, place, unit, hand tracking, threshold) never touches its stock, and the edit sheet and `PUT /api/ingredients/{id}` have no stock. — `AddIngredient`, `EditIngredient`, `IngredientSheet`
- **P11** Removing an ingredient deletes it with its stock. Once recipes exist, one that a recipe uses will be refused instead. — `RemoveIngredient`
- **P12** The pantry lists every ingredient by name, ignoring case. On screen a place is chosen with the Fridge / Freezer / Pantry / All control; ingredients that are out sit dimmed at the bottom of their place. Typing in the search field filters across all places, and when no ingredient has exactly that name it offers to add it. — `IngredientQueries`, `PantryPage`
- **P13** The kitchen is shared: everyone who is signed in sees and edits every ingredient (see [accounts.md](accounts.md) A3). An unknown ingredient answers 404, a broken rule 422. — `NotFound`, `DomainExceptionListener`
- **P14** Known limit: there are no row locks, so two requests that change the same ingredient within milliseconds can still lose one change, and the last absolute write wins. This is accepted for two people; an `AtomicChange` with a row lock is the upgrade path.
